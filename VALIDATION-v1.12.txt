Scope and limits:
The browser tests mock weather responses, tile images and some audio/device
interfaces. Radio tests use fake Chromium microphone streams. Bluetooth/HID
inventory and button events, TTS and Android bridge callbacks are simulated.
These tests do not validate physical microphones, real headsets, real cellular
networks, actual GPS accuracy, native background radio or production load.
The wind colours rank ambient wind-component magnitude, not measured drag.
No Render deployment or independent security audit was performed.
No native APK/EXE installer was rebuilt. See RELEASE-v1.12.md for limitations.
The existing regression harnesses were adapted for account cookies and scoped
profile keys; the hot-unplug test removes the mock device BEFORE emitting its
track-ended event, and the wind assertion examines the full expanded panel.

Verify sign-in on a second real device and repeat after a Render redeploy.
Verify radio and alerts audibly with the rider/escort hardware. Back up data.
